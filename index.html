Serve the midnight crowd

Read each customer ticket, tap ingredients to build exactly that order, then press Serve the ticket. Correct orders earn cash and build a streak. A wrong wrap costs patience; a customer who leaves costs the shift. Reach $620 before the 90-second clock runs out. If the street cat appears, press Shoo! quickly to protect your fries.
